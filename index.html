What happens during a power cut?

Keeping devices on needs the right equipment, a battery, and those devices connected for backup.

On-grid

Most standard systems switch off during a power cut for safety. Solar panels alone do not keep the lights on.

Off-grid

A battery saves extra daytime solar for later. The amount you can use depends on the battery size and weather.

Hybrid

Can use your usual electricity supply and a battery. Only devices connected to the backup outlets stay on.

Questions worth asking

Will solar panels keep my home on during a power cut?

Not automatically. It depends on the inverter and how the system is wired. Ask your installer which devices have backup, whether a battery is included, and whether it can handle devices starting up.

Can I send extra solar electricity to the grid in Pakistan?

Grid export can require utility approval, compatible equipment, and a suitable meter. Requirements and payment rules can change, so check the latest NEPRA notices and ask your local electricity provider before buying a system.

What should I check before choosing a solar installer?

Ask for a written quote listing equipment models, expected output and its assumptions, battery and backup details, installation and protection work, warranties, and after-sales support. The U.S. Department of Energy homeowner’s guide has general questions to help compare proposals; local approvals still depend on Pakistani requirements.

Can an off-grid system work without a battery?

Solar panels can power devices while the sun is shining. At night or when sunlight is low, you need a battery or another power source.
